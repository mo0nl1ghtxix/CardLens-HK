What Day 1 does not show

  • What buyers in Hong Kong actually paid. The sample holds asking prices only.
  • Japan-domestic prices (SNKRDUNK, Mercari, Yahoo Auctions). The "Japanese card" prices above come from the US import market.
  • A condition adjustment. The US prices are for Near Mint copies.
  • Short-term hype after a release. Newly released cards can sit well above landed cost for weeks.
